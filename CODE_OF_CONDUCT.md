# Code of conduct

## Our commitment

OwlPath aims to support a respectful, rigorous, and inclusive research-software community. Participants are expected to communicate in ways that are professional, understandable, and safe for people with different clinical, technical, cultural, and language backgrounds.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss ideas and evidence without attacking people.
- State uncertainty, limitations, and conflicts of interest honestly.
- Welcome careful questions from non-specialists.
- Give credit for contributions and sources.
- Protect confidential information, credentials, and health data.
- Separate verified results from hypotheses, demonstrations, and future plans.
- Challenge medical or technical claims with evidence and specific reasoning.
- Accept correction and repair mistakes promptly.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, or sexualized conduct.
- Publishing personal information, patient information, credentials, or private correspondence without permission.
- Fabricating results, citations, test status, authorship, affiliations, or clinical validation.
- Pressuring others to approve unsafe, misleading, or non-reproducible work.
- Using project channels for individualized diagnosis or treatment advice.
- Retaliating against good-faith security, privacy, reproducibility, or research-integrity concerns.

## Scope

This code applies to repository discussions, reviews, meetings, demonstrations, issue trackers, and other spaces where a person represents OwlPath.

## Reporting and enforcement

For ordinary conduct concerns, open a GitHub Issue containing no confidential information. For concerns that also involve a security or privacy vulnerability, use GitHub Private Vulnerability Reporting. Do not include patient data, credentials, exploit details, or unnecessary personal detail in public channels.

The project owner is the accountable maintainer for `v0.1.x`; public attribution remains **OwlPath Project Team**. Responses may include clarification, correction, content removal, a warning, temporary restriction, or exclusion from project spaces, proportionate to the behavior and risk.

This document is project-authored and does not imply adoption by any unconfirmed institution.
