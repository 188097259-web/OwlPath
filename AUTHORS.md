**OwlPath Project Team**

The project founder and clinical lead is the founder of **DR.ECC**. Their verified real-world identity has been disclosed to the competition organizers. The public repository intentionally does not display their individual name, institutional affiliation, private email address, phone number, or other personal contact details.

## Authorship and data-governance principles

- Authorship must reflect substantial, accountable contributions and approval of the released work.
- Source-control activity alone does not automatically establish academic authorship.
- Clinical data access, dataset authorship, software authorship, and manuscript authorship are distinct roles.
- Analysis authorization does not imply permission to publicly name an institution or redistribute its data.
- Automated tools and language models are not listed as human authors. Material use should be disclosed where required by a competition, journal, institution, or research protocol.
